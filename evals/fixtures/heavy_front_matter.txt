CHAPTER I

THE GARDEN

Once upon a time there was a small brown rabbit who lived beneath a
hedge at the bottom of a long garden.

Every morning he looked through the gate at the carrot patch, and every
morning the gardener chased him away.

One evening the rabbit squeezed under the gate and ate three carrots,
and could not squeeze back again.

In the morning the gardener found him and carried him home in a
wheelbarrow, and that was the end of the matter.
