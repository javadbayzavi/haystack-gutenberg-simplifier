THE RABBIT AND THE CARROT


Once upon a time there was a small brown rabbit who lived beneath a
hedge at the bottom of a long garden.

Every morning the rabbit looked through the gate at the carrot patch,
and every morning the gardener chased him away with a rake.

One evening, when the gardener had gone indoors for his supper, the
rabbit squeezed under the gate and ate three carrots, one after another,
until he could eat no more.

He was so full that he could not squeeze back under the gate, and he had
to wait until morning, when the gardener found him and carried him home
in a wheelbarrow.

The rabbit never ate three carrots again. Two, sometimes. Never three.
