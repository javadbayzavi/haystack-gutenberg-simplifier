A MANUAL OF BEEKEEPING APPARATUS


SECTION 1. SMOKERS

1.1 The bellows smoker consists of a firebox, a bellows, and a nozzle.
Table 1 gives the recommended nozzle diameters by hive size.

  Hive size     Nozzle diameter
  Small         12 mm
  Medium        16 mm
  Large         20 mm

1.2 Fuel should be dry and free of resin. Pine needles, burlap, and
untreated wood shavings are all suitable. Do not use treated timber.

SECTION 2. HIVE TOOLS

2.1 The standard hive tool is a flat steel bar 250 mm in length with a
hooked end for lifting frames.

2.2 Sterilise tools between apiaries. See Appendix C for the approved
solutions and their concentrations.

APPENDIX C. STERILISING SOLUTIONS

  Sodium hypochlorite    0.5 per cent
  Ethanol                70 per cent
